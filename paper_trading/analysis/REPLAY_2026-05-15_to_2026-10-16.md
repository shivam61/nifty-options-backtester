## Method (what the replay does)

- **Decision for trading day D (11:30 IST)** uses only D-1 data: Nifty close and India VIX close of D-1, and D-1 5-day return / VIX 5-day change. Expiry = `get_weekly_expiry_in_range(D, 3, 8)` (holiday-aware, `data/nse_holidays.json` plus the closures found in the cache rows). Strategy rule and `should_enter` gates are the classes used by `signal-combined` (`strategies/weekly_strategies.py`). Strikes come from `generate_legs` at the D-1 close, as the CLI would print them.
- **Entry fill** on D = `nifty_mid_session` (open + 0.40 x (close - open)) with the weekly 0.75x slippage model (`backtester/fills.py` `ConservativeFillModel`, same code the engine uses), priced at D-1 VIX. Gap penalty uses the D vs D-1 move. Mid-session spot is used only for the fill, as specified.
- **Positions**: 15 lots x 65. At most one open weekly position (the engine's rule). A Mon/Tue signal while a position is open is logged as SKIP OPEN_POSITION.
- **Exits** are checked on each later trading day's close, marked by BS with that day's close and VIX, and realised with the full exit slippage model. Order of checks: expiry (dte <= 0), short-strike breach (close beyond a short strike), stop (mark loss >= 100% of credit), PCS profit target (mark >= 75% of credit), IC max hold (4 trading days). Otherwise hold to expiry. The exit fill is assumed at 15:15 IST (operator practice), not at the close itself.
- **Costs**: brokerage Rs500 per trade (flat, as in the journal's simulated Trade 1). Statutory charges (STT, exchange, SEBI, stamp, GST) are not in the Rs500. Using the repo's CostModel, they would add about Rs134 per trade on average (estimate only, not subtracted).
- **Capital_Deployed_Rs** in trades.csv is a proxy (max loss at the spread, width minus credit, times 975). No margin calculator was available.
- **Account equity** = Rs25,00,000 plus realised net P&L. Open MTM is only in the Notes, never in equity.

## Sensitivity: strikes set at the mid-session spot (not the primary)

The primary run sets strikes and gates from the D-1 close. A variant (`DECISION_SPOT=mid`, outputs in `sensitivity_mid/`) sets strikes from the mid-session spot, which is closer to what the live CLI would print at 11:30 but uses D's intraday price. It gives 19 trades, total Rs-54,915, mean Rs-2,890 per trade, win rate 57.9%. So the loss is not caused by the prev-close strike choice; the stop and breach exits drive it.

Note on `Slippage_vs_Backtest_bp` in trades.csv: it is (fill credit - credit at the D-1 close strikes) / that credit. Because strikes are fixed at the D-1 close and the fill is at mid-session, this column measures the intraday spot move, not execution slippage. Read it as a spot-gap column.

**Engine defect found (not fixed; main checkout untouched).** In `CombinedBacktestEngine._process_weekly_exit` the strategy object is re-selected each day with `_select_weekly_strategy(entry_vix, today's market_data)`. An IC opened when |5-day return| < 2% can be re-selected as PCS on a later day when |5-day return| >= 2%. Then `WeeklyPutCreditSpread.update_premiums` reprices the call legs as puts. Example: trade entered 2026-06-02 (IC, 23800CE/24100CE, 22700PE/22400PE, spot 23,331). On the 2026-06-02 close the call legs were marked at 375.68 and 617.23 (BS call at 23,484 with 7 DTE is about 93 and 36) and the trade booked +Rs283k in a day. Its maximum profit is its credit: about 62.9 per unit x 975 = Rs61k, from the engine's own entry premiums (68.43, 27.42, 35.90, 14.02; see dbg.log in the sandbox). The as-shipped engine weekly P&L is Rs579,089 vs Rs-28,942 patched, a gap of Rs608,031. The three IC trades with |P&L| > Rs1.5 lakh as shipped (entries 2026-06-02, 2026-07-21, 2026-10-13) total Rs642,036 as shipped vs Rs41,307 patched, i.e. Rs600,729 of the gap. The 5-day return at the 2026-06-02 close was -2.28%, so the trade was re-priced as a PCS that day. The patched run pins the exit strategy to the entry strategy. Any engine weekly P&L from periods with IC trades may be overstated. Worth checking before quoting the 17-year weekly numbers.

**Why the replay and the engine differ (even after the patch):**
1. Strategy rule: the live CLI uses PCS if VIX <= 16, else IC (what this replay follows). The engine uses IC if VIX is in 12-22 and |5-day return| < 2%, else PCS. Strategy mix: replay 15 PCS / 4 IC; engine (patched) 7 PCS / 7 IC.
2. Timing: the engine decides on D's close and fills at D+1 mid-session (its `_pending_weekly_entry`). The CLI decides at 11:30 on D and the replay fills on D. This shifts entries by a day (e.g. replay 2026-05-18 vs engine 2026-05-19).
3. Strikes: the engine picks strikes at the fill-day spot. The replay picks them at the D-1 close.
4. Exits: the engine uses `check_weekly_exit` (redesigned policy: trailing delta, DTE <= 1 exit, trailing peak/drop) and closes at theoretical marks with statutory costs. The replay uses the Phase-1 rules (stop 100%, breach, PCS 75% target, IC 4-day max, hold to expiry) at executable exit prices, and Rs500 brokerage.
5. Position sizing: the engine sizes dynamically (lots 13-15 across its as-shipped trades). The replay is fixed at 15 lots.
6. Gates: the engine applies event-calendar blocks (18 blocked days in this window) and a 4-loss cooldown (triggered 0 times here). The CLI applies neither, so the replay does not. The engine's DTE check is at the fill date (0 drops here).
7. Cadence: both allow one weekly position at a time, so one entry sometimes blocks the next Monday/Tuesday. The engine's blocking depends on its own exit dates.

## Honesty notes

- **Models.** The model cutoff is 2026-08-28 (stated by the user): May-Aug is in-sample and Sep-Oct out-of-sample. **For the weekly track this does not matter.** In Phase 1 with the risk engine OFF, weekly entries and exits are rule-based (`should_enter`, fixed expiry rule, fixed exits). The entry model (`entry_model_v4.pkl`) and exit model (`exit_model.pkl`) are not consulted for any weekly decision in this replay. The monthly track, the only consumer of those models, is not simulated.
- **Prices are estimates.** Every premium is Black-Scholes with the repo's VIX-to-IV smile (`iv_from_vix`), not a market quote. Real fills may differ, especially on stop days when spreads widen.
- **Daily-close exits.** Stops, breaches and targets are checked on daily closes, not intraday. Intraday breaches could exit earlier and at worse prices. Several stops overshot the 100% level (e.g. RP-006, RP-008), because the close mark was below the stop line already.
- **Min hold.** The CLI prints 'PCS Min Hold 2 trading days'. The stated exit spec does not include it, so it is not enforced. Every PCS profit target in this replay fired on day 2 or later, so it never bound.
- **Holidays.** `data/nse_holidays.json` lists only 2026-10-20 onward. Four weekday closures in May-Oct (2026-05-28, 06-26, 09-14, 10-02) were inferred from missing data rows. No weekly expiry fell on any of them.
- **Not modelled**: margin, statutory costs beyond the Rs500 flat assumption, intraday monitoring, partial fills, order rejections, the account-pause rule (proposed, unconfirmed; the replay never reached 15% DD), and any monthly-track interaction.
